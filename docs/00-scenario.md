# Scenario: Contoso Energy Home

Fictional company. All names, numbers and documents are invented.

## Customers
- Consumers (B2C): setup and warranty questions
- Installer partners (B2B): on-site technical incidents, higher priority
- Enterprise customers: multi-site contracts with SLA

## Service tiers
| Tier | First response | Resolution | Hours |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard | 8 h | 5 business days | Mon-Fri 9-18 |
| Premium | 4 h | 3 business days | Mon-Fri 8-20 |
| Partner / Enterprise | 1 h | 1 business day | 24/7 for critical |

## Queues
Tier 1 General, Tier 2 Technical (inverters), Partner Desk.

## Typical cases
- Thermostat will not join Wi-Fi
- Inverter shows an error code
- Warranty replacement request
- Complaint about an installation

## Features demonstrated (in order)
1. SLA
2. Agent Scripts
3. Customer Service workspace
4. Email intake agent (AI)
